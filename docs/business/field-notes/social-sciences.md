# Field notes — social sciences & education

Companion to `census-methodology.md`, which stays general. This file holds only what is true of
**social sciences and education** and would be noise in the general method. Read both before working
a social-sciences row; add what you learn here, not there.

Started 2026-09-14. Sections 1–6 are the pilot researcher's report from phase-2 wave 1 (30 rows, one
Opus agent, **10 searches, 21 of 30 rows at zero searches**). There was no index pass before this
wave: unlike CS/AI and engineering, this field has almost no deposit-index coverage, so the pilot
started from the society artefacts.

## The unit, the bar, and the scope

Accepted contributed presentations = **papers + posters accepted for one edition**; invited,
plenary, keynote, presidential and award presentations excluded; roundtables and author-meets-critics
with no submitted papers excluded; withdrawn excluded. ≥1,500 counted = VERIFIED; 800–1,499 =
BORDERLINE; below 800 **on positive evidence** = OUT. A stated total never beats a countable
artefact. Inferred or organiser-stated never reaches VERIFIED.

**THE PANEL RULE IS THE WHOLE BALL GAME IN THIS FIELD** (Rupert, settled 2026-09-12). Social-science
programmes are built from panels and organised sessions, not poster halls. Each paper inside a panel
counts as one. Every artefact in this wave supports this directly — papers are individually titled
records with their own authors inside every one of the counted programmes. Counting sessions instead
of papers would understate this field by **3–4×**: ECPR is 2,077 papers in 539 panels, LASA 4,609 in
1,671, APSA 5,570 in 1,295, ASSA 2,210 in 478.

## 2. Every route that worked

### 2.1 The society WordPress media index — the workhorse of this field

Five rows, zero searches each. The pattern lists programme PDFs that the site's own navigation does
not link:

```
https://<society>/wp-json/wp/v2/media?search=<term>&per_page=50&_fields=source_url,date
```

Useful `search` terms, in yield order: `program`, `annual meeting`, `convention`, `program book`,
`preliminary`, `abstract`, `schedule`.

| Society | Host | What it yields |
|---|---|---|
| **APSA** | `apsanet.org` | Full Annual Meeting programme for 2024, 2025, 2026 |
| **ASA** | `www.asanet.org` | Programme books 1999, 2000, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2022 — **nothing newer is public** |
| **ASC** | `asc41.org` | Full programme every year 2021–2025, plus a historic run back to 1958 |
| **NCA** | `www.natcom.org` | Convention programme 2024 and 2025 |
| **AAG** | `www.aag.org` | Annual meeting programme 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 |


**ASC is the trap worth remembering.** `https://asc41.org/wp-json/` returns **HTTP 406** to a bare
request, which reads as "not WordPress". It *is* WordPress — the page markup exposes
`/wp-json/oembed/1.0/embed` and `/wp-content/` — and the media endpoint answers normally when given
a desktop Chrome user-agent **and** an `Accept: application/json` header. Always check the markup
before concluding a society is not on WordPress.

**AAG has a second trap:** its 2025 and 2026 files are named `AAG-2025-program-missing-abstract-titles.pdf`
and `AAG-2026-working-program-missing-abstract-titles.pdf` — incomplete by the organiser's own
filename. The most recent COMPLETE book is `AAG-2024-PDF-program-FINAL.pdf`.

### 2.2 Counting a two-column programme PDF by its own typography

**This is the single most important technique in this field.** `pdftotext` alone cannot delimit
papers in any of these books: they are two-column, titles wrap over several lines, and a fixed-column
split fragments the right column (tried on ASC — the gutter drifts between columns 50 and 58 and most
pages have no clean gutter at all). The layout *is* the data. Use:

```
pdftohtml -xml -i -stdout <book>.pdf > all.xml
```

Font ids are **global** in that mode (they are re-assigned per page if you use `-f`/`-l`, so always
run the whole document). Each `<text top= left= font=>` element then carries the role.

| Book | Discriminator | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **APSA 2026** (487 pp) | font 268 = labels (`Chair:` 1,528, `Papers:` 1,295, `Disc:` 1,183, `Part:` 301), 257 = paper-title words (one element per word), 256 = author lines and session numbers, 254 = DIVISION heads. **A paper = one maximal run of font-257 inside a `Papers:` block** | **5,570** in 1,295 sessions |
| **ASA 2022** (180 pp) | the **left coordinate encodes the role**: 40/41 = first line of a paper in a paper session, 62 = first line of a paper at a roundtable table, 51 = `Table Presider:`, 78/84 = wrapped continuation (mirrored at 472/473, 494, 483, 510/516) | 2,693 blocks, **2,644** carrying the `Title - Author` separator |

| **LASA 2026** (307 pp) | paper titles are **italic** (Montserrat-LightItalic, font 3); every label, session header, session title and author line is regular (font 2) | **4,609** in 1,671 sessions |
| **ESA 2024** (1,401 pp) | font 5 (30pt) = session header, **15 (24pt) = abstract title**, 16 (17pt) = author name, 2 (14pt) = affiliation and body | **3,450** |
| **SPSP 2025** (86 pp) | a four-column table whose **rightmost column (x = 1083) is the organiser's own Category** — Poster / Preconference / Symposia / \*Roundtable / Data Blitz / Professional Development / \*Event / Symposia;Featured Session | **2,265** main-convention |

**ASC needs one extra step that none of the others do.** `pdftohtml` emits **several `<text>` runs per
visual line** whenever the font changes mid-line, so the raw indent histogram is noise. Merge runs
into lines first — round `top` to a ~7-unit grid within each column and concatenate by `left` — and
the hierarchy appears: left column 70 = label or session header, 81 = first line of an entry, 91/92 =
continuation; right column 367 / 378 / 388–389 the same. Then a paper is one entry at 81/378 plus its
continuations, kept only if it carries ASC's `Title; Author, Affiliation` semicolon. **3,010** in 709
sessions.

**AAG needs no geometry at all**, because its book is not really two-column at the entry level: each
session prints the literal line `Presentations (if applicable) and Session Agenda:` and then one line
per presentation laid out as presenter | title. A presentation is a line inside that block beginning
at column 0 **and** carrying a second column (`^\S.*?\s{3,}\S`), closing on
`^(Non-Presenting Participants|Type:|Sponsor Group|Primary Organizer:|Primary Session Chair)`. The
two-column requirement is load-bearing: it removes exactly 1,077 following-session title lines
against 1,085 blocks. **3,283** presentations.


**NCA needs no geometry either, because it marks papers typographically**: every contributed paper is
a curly-quoted title followed immediately by its authors, while panellists appear as bare names under
a `Presenters:` label. Count on whitespace-collapsed text, per page:

```
“([^“”]{10,400})”\s*([A-Z][A-Za-z.’'\-]+(?:\s+[A-Z][A-Za-z.’'\-]+){0,3},|[A-Z][A-Za-z.’'\-]+\s+[A-Z][A-Za-z.’'\-]+;)
```

The author-follows constraint is what excludes the front matter's quoted prose (without it the bare
quote count is 2,477/2,567 and includes the president's welcome). **2,248** (`-raw`).

**PAA needs neither**, because it numbers every oral paper `<session>-<n>` in the margin. Count unique
ids with two different anchors on the two extraction modes — `(?:^|\s{2,})([A-Z]?\d{1,3}-\d{1,3})\s{2,}\S`
on `-layout` and `(?m)^\s*([A-Z]?\d{1,3}-\d{1,3})\s+\S` on `-raw`. Both give **1,106**.

### 2.3 Programme-platform data files and backing APIs

**None of these five is a rung-5 job**, and three of them look like one at first glance.

**JSM — a static JSON export behind a 5 KB shell.** The programme page footer says "data from the
Attendee Interactive API"; its client script `assets/app2.js?v=62` names four open endpoints:

```
https://ww2.amstat.org/meetings/jsm/<year>/onlineprogram/data/stats.json?v=62     # {"sessions":910,"talks":2873}
https://ww2.amstat.org/meetings/jsm/<year>/onlineprogram/data/search.json?v=62    # 1.9 MB, every session
```

`search.json` carries `type` and `talkCount` per session, i.e. **the organiser's own invited/
contributed split for free**: Contributed Papers 1,091 + Topic-Contributed 621 + Contributed Posters
337 + Contributed Speed 129 = **2,178**, against Invited Paper Session 598 and Invited Posters 40.

**ISA — the Confex backing API.** The SPA front end is irrelevant; the API is open:

```
https://isaconf.confex.com/isaconf/<meeting>/meetingapi.cgi/Paper     # use --compressed
https://isaconf.confex.com/isaconf/<meeting>/meetingapi.cgi/Program
```


**The meeting code must be probed, not guessed**: `world2023`, `isa2023`, `melbourne2023`, `wcs2023`
and `congress2023` all 404; `wc2023` and `wc2027` return 200. Records expose `Withdrawn`, `Type`,
`ProgramCode` and `MeetingTitle`, so the whole exclusion is done on the organiser's own fields.

**ECPR — three DevExpress ArrayStores embedded in the page.** `…/Events/AcademicProgramme/Programme?EventID=<n>`
is 1.47 MB and *looks* like a JS grid with no paper links anywhere in the visible markup. The whole
programme is inline as `ArrayStore({"data":[…]})` literals — 75 sections, 539 panels, **2,077 papers**
(each with `ID`, `EventID`, `Title`, `People`, `Keywords`, optional `AttachmentID`). **Extract with a
balanced-bracket, string-aware scan, not a regex**: a naive regex matched the 75-record sections store
instead. Get the EventID from `https://ecpr.eu/generalconference`.

**EERA/ECER — EERA's own TYPO3 abstracts database**, not ConfTool as the brief anticipated:

```
https://eera-ecer.de/ecer-programmes                                        # the search form carries both id spaces
https://eera-ecer.de/ecer-programmes/conference/<conf>/network/<net>        # listing, with an <ol> of contributions
https://eera-ecer.de/ecer-programmes/conference/<conf>/contribution/<id>    # one presentation
```

Network ids 875–916 (875 = "01. Professional Learning and Development" … 908 = "34. Citizenship
Education", plus 909 Functions, 910 Additional events, 911 Emerging Researchers' Group, 912 Governance
Meetings, 913 Central & EERA Sessions, 914 Bursaries, 915 Blocked, 916 preview); conference ids one per
year 1998–2026. **3,582** contributions, **3,216** after removing the ERC pre-conference and
central/governance networks.

**AAR — a public program book on a submission host.** `papers.aarweb.org` requires a login to *submit*
but not to read:

```
https://papers.aarweb.org/program-book/<node>?page=0..73     # 32290 = November 2026 meeting
```

Every paper is its own `<article data-history-node-id="NNNNNN" class="node node--type-paper …">`
carrying presenter, title and abstract, inline in the listing. Count **distinct node ids**: 1,396
across 299 papers-sessions, 209 roundtable-sessions and 159 other-events.


**AEA/ASSA — enumerate by session id, because the pager is broken.** Every pagination variant returns
the same 50 sessions (§3). Instead:

```
https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/<year>/program/<id>        # papers are class="paper-title"
```

Crawl a generous id range with a resumable script. Ids resolve only at **1000–2458**; the empty
margins on both sides (600–999 and 2459–2700 all 404) are what proves the range complete. A second
pass over the 478 paper-bearing sessions recovers each session's `type` and `Hosted By:` association.
**2,210** papers; 72 distinct host associations.

### 2.4 Crossref proceedings containers

Coverage in this field is thin — ANPEd, CNIE, LASA, ICME, ISA and ECER deposit **nothing** — but where
it exists it is exact and free.

```
# the facet IS the figure; query.container-title only re-ranks
https://api.crossref.org/works?filter=type:proceedings-article,prefix:<PREFIX>,from-pub-date:<Y>-01-01,until-pub-date:<Y>-12-31&rows=0&facet=container-title:200
# then re-derive by cursor-paging and grouping, to be safe
https://api.crossref.org/works?filter=…&rows=1000&select=DOI,container-title,title&cursor=*
```

| Body | Member / prefix | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **AERA** | 1604 / `10.3102` | `Proceedings of the 2025 AERA Annual Meeting` **4,676**; 2024 4,189; 2023 4,678 |
| **IATED** | 8703 / `10.21125` | 2025: ICERI **1,619** / EDULEARN 1,696 / INTED 1,255, summing exactly to the prefix total 4,570 |
| **Even3** | 11875 / `10.29327` | A **Brazilian anais platform depositing one DOI per trabalho for many congresses** — 13,848 proceedings items in 2025 alone. CONIC = **1,125**, confirmed by DOI slug `10.29327/xxxiii-conic.` |

**Even3 is the find to carry forward**: its year-scoped container facet is a one-call census of a large
slice of Brazilian conference output. The same 2025 facet also shows XLII Congresso Brasileiro de
Reumatologia 741, Anais SIMPEP 551, SBAI+SBSE 546, SOBER 491 — phase-1 material for other fields.


### 2.5 National and society platforms

- **J-STAGE for Japan.** JPA's proceedings are `cdjournal=pacjpa` (日本心理学会大会発表論文集, eISSN
  2433-7609), **one volume per convention**, and `&vol=` filters correctly:
  ```
  https://api.jstage.jst.go.jp/searchapi/do?service=3&cdjournal=pacjpa&vol=<N>&start=1&count=200
  ```

  requested volume.
- **Higher Logic society sites** (PAA). The programme book sits on S3, linked from a `/final-program`

  Returns `content-type: application/force-download`, so check the file type. **fno order is not title

## 4. Counting rules this field needs

1. **Report papers, never sessions.** See *The unit* above. This is the field's defining rule.
2. **Exclude by the organiser's own label wherever one exists, and say so.** JSM types its sessions
   Invited / Contributed / Topic-Contributed; ISA's API carries `Withdrawn`, `Type` and `ProgramCode`;
   SPSP's table has a Category column; ANPEd labels every entry's type; EEA-ESEM types each session.
   Where **no** label exists — AEA/ASSA marks no paper invited anywhere — **say plainly that invited
   content could not be separated** rather than implying it was.
3. **Withdrawals are real and large.** ISA flags 2,128 of 6,765 records withdrawn; counting the raw
   total would overstate that row by 46%. Always look for a withdrawal flag before counting an API.

4. **Roundtables, panels and author-meets-critics carry no submitted papers** and are structurally
   separate in every programme here: APSA `Part:` (301), AAR roundtable-sessions (209), ASA
   `Panelists:` / `Author:` / `Critics:`, NCA `Presenters:`, AAG's 341 zero-presentation sessions,
   JSM's Invited/Topic-Contributed **Panel** sessions (all `talkCount` 0). Excluding them is
   mechanical, not a judgement.
5. **Two extraction modes must agree, or the disagreement is resolved on STRUCTURE, never averaged.**
   NCA: `-raw` 2,248 vs `-layout` 1,996 — all 302 raw-only titles are present in the layout text too
   and were missed only because the two-column spread separates a title from its authors, so **`-raw`
   wins**. PAA and KPA agreed exactly in both modes, and that agreement *is* the validation.
6. **Match a union on a normalised title PREFIX, never the full string.** NCA's naive full-string union
   is 2,581 and inflated by wrap variants; matched on a 30-, 40- or 60-character normalised prefix it
   is a stable 2,296–2,298.
7. **Check union against sum before summing sub-listings.** ECER's 42 networks: union = sum = 3,582,
   so nothing is cross-listed. COMIE's 18 areas likewise (1,250 = 1,250). The engineering precedent
   (CIGRE, 8 joint papers double-counted) is why this must be checked rather than assumed.
8. **Poster and oral tracks are routinely in different artefacts — say which one you counted.**
   PAA's book has 1,106 orals and *states* 981 posters it never lists; APSA's book lists 36 iPoster
   sessions by title and time only, with the posters online; KPA's abstract book is **posters only**
   (561) while the orals sit in a separate programme book. In every such case the figure is a FLOOR
   and must be labelled one.
9. **Prove page coverage by overrunning the pager.** AAR: pages 0–73, and the 70-page partial crawl
   (1,291 papers) was caught and closed to 74 pages (1,396). ANPEd: pages 35–39 come back empty.
   ASSA: ids 600–999 and 2459–2700 all 404. **An overrun that returns nothing is the proof**; stopping
   at the last non-empty page is not.

10. **Sanity-check papers per session.** 4.30 (APSA), 4.53 (ASA), 4.25 (ASC), 4.63 (ASSA), 4.67 (AAR),
    4.4 (AAG), 3.93 (IPSA), 3.85 (ECPR), 3.7 (ASA roundtable tables). A mis-parse shows up here first —
    ASA's first attempt gave 1.85 and was wrong (it had missed the roundtable indent level and the
    ±1px drift in the base-left column).
11. **A stated figure whose own breakdown reconciles is worth far more than a bare quoted total.**
    CPS's report gives 1,554 presented papers *and* itemises 1 + 6 + 307 + 159 + 436 + 645, which sums
    to exactly 1,554 — so the invited exclusion (7) becomes arithmetic. Still `REPORTED BY SOURCE, NOT
    COUNTED`, still BORDERLINE, but auditable.
12. **Separate submissions from acceptances explicitly.** PAA "some 3,700 submissions" vs ~2,030
    programmed; CPS 3,071 有效论文摘要 received vs 1,554 交流; ISA 6,765 records vs 4,637 live. Never

## 5. Traps

- **Acronym collisions bit twice inside one 30-row queue.** **APSA** is the American Political Science
  Association (row 6, 5,570 papers) *and* the unresolvable "Asia-Pacific Sociological Association"
  (row 162). **ESA** here is the European Sociological Association, not the Ecological Society of
  America on the physical-sciences sheet — a search returned the Ecological Society's "Call for
  Contributed Abstracts" and it was discarded. Also live in this field per the brief: ASA (sociology,
  not anesthesiology/acoustics/agronomy), ISA (international studies *and* sociology), APA
  (psychological, not psychiatric), AAS, SRA, ICA, BPS, AHA, ECE, ECP, IEA. **Confirm the society's own
  domain before citing any page.**
- **Search snippets were WRONG ON REAL NUMBERS, twice.** IPSA: the snippet said "3,450 participants"
  and "3,059 papers"; the page itself says **3,370 and 3,053**. ICME: a snippet attributed delegate and
  programme figures to `icme15.org`, which is now a gambling squatter. Neither snippet figure is
  recorded anywhere in the results. This is the clearest vindication of "open the page" in the census
  so far.

- **Sister meetings share one platform.** ISA's Confex host serves both `wc2023` (World Congress,
  quadrennial) and `forum2025` (ISA Forum, quadrennial, offset). IATED runs ICERI, EDULEARN and INTED
  off one Crossref prefix. NCTM runs four separate event families with four separate attendance
  projections in one media kit (Annual Meeting 6,000; Spring Conferences 2,000 each; NCTE-NCTM Joint
  1,300; Interactive Institute 500). **Never merge, never substitute.**
- **One publisher can deposit the same meeting twice.** AERA deposits its programme *and* its Online
  Paper Repository under **interleaved submission ids** (proc 2178471–2253013, ip 2178470–2257239);
  1,375 ids recur, so summing the two containers double-counts. Crossref also lists each IATED
  container twice, year-stamped and generic, with identical counts.
- **`?page=N` fails in three distinct ways in this field, so always fetch page 2 and diff it against
  page 1**: Cloudflare challenges the query string but not the bare path (EEA-ESEM); the parameter is
  silently ignored and every page returns identical rows (AEA `/preliminary`, and COMIE's `&p=`); or it
  works perfectly (AAR, ANPEd).
- **Local numeric conventions.** Chinese 篇 = papers, 人 = people, 场 = sessions, **人次 = person-times**:
  CPS's `线上实际参会超过4.5万人次` is 45,000 person-*times* of online viewing, **not** 45,000 attendees,
  and was not used as attendance. Korean 초록집 = abstract book, 프로그램북 = programme book, 구두 = oral,
  포스터 = poster. Japanese 大会発表論文集 = convention proceedings, 正誤訂正一覧 = errata.
- **Regex over-matching in CJK.** An exclusion regex on `プログラム` caught genuine JPA paper titles
  (`…自伝プログラムの効果`, `…予防プログラム`) — 21 false positives. Anchor CJK exclusions to **whole
  titles** (`^\s*(正誤訂正一覧|目次|表紙|奥付|索引)\s*$`), which leaves exactly one real exclusion.
- **Practitioner conventions are a different animal and the character judgement decides them.** NCTM's
  own media kit sells an audience of classroom teachers and district purchasers who come "to get new
  ideas for teaching" and "visit the exhibit hall for over an hour"; its sessions are 60–75 minute
  workshops delivered by one or two teachers, so each session yields about **one** presentation, not
  four. That structural difference is precisely why its judged ratio is **14%** against DGPs' **80%** —
  and why a ratio must never be copied across from one meeting to another.
- **Spam-injected but genuine hosts.** `anais.anped.org.br` carries injected Turkish gambling links yet
  serves the society's real Drupal proceedings. Don't discard the host; don't follow the links.
- **Credential walls are not rung 5.** IPSA's proceedings library, and the ASA/ASC member archives. A
  browser gains nothing — do not queue them.
- **A "PDF" URL may not be a PDF, and a 200 may not be a page.** IPSA's programme excerpt (HTML catch-all
  at 261,910 bytes), ANPEd's homepage (a 403 body inside a 200), the uni.lu press kit (202, zero bytes).
  **Check status AND content-type AND size before counting anything.**
